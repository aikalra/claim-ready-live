05 - Why it works

Pixels stay. Meaning travels.

The site agent runs detection and tracking on the existing NVR/VMS server or a small edge box. What crosses is anonymous event metadata - kilobytes an hour, not gigabytes of video. Footage stays on-site; no inbound port or centralized video store.

The corpus is invented.

Real incident footage is rare and legally radioactive. The generator writes millions of labeled incidents - slips, thefts, intrusions - and the grader pre-trains on those. It ships already knowing what a liability-relevant event looks like.

Records, not dashboards.

Every grade lands as a signed, claim-ready incident record: what happened, whose liability, what it costs, evidence by pointer. Built for the CFO and the insurer - GL, liquor liability, property - where the budget already exists.

06 - Why venues buy it

Coverage without headcount.

Every camera becomes a watcher that never blinks. Incidents get caught, graded, and written up while your team keeps working - no control room, no review shifts, no footage queue.

Time is the evidence.

The 4D track is what separates a real fall from a staged claim: where the person was, how fast they moved, what changed in the seconds before the event. Every record carries the full trajectory, not a single frame.

The claim file lands first.

Every incident ships as a signed, auditable record - what happened, the grade, the evidence pointer, the cost band. It reaches the CFO and the insurer while the other side is still requesting footage.
